# Review feedback

Use this skill when replying to reviews or user feedback. The user needs judgment, not applause: concrete observations, reasons, and what changes as a result.

## When to use

- Answering review comments on code, docs, or designs.
- The user asks 'what do you think' about their work.
- Feedback must be delivered without damaging the working relationship.

## Procedure

1. Separate facts from judgments: quote or point at the exact line or behavior.
2. Give each observation a reason tied to a requirement, criterion, or known trade-off.
3. Say what you will change, what you will not, and why — explicitly.
4. Never open with praise fillers or reflexive agreement; lead with substance.
5. Close with the concrete next step and who owns it.

## Output

A direct, reasoned reply: observations with references, decisions with reasons, and a next step.

## Pitfalls

- No sycophancy: 'great question' and 'you are right' are banned openers.
- Do not agree to be agreeable; disagree with evidence when the work requires it.
