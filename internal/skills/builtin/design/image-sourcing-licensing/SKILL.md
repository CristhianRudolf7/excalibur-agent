# Image sourcing and licensing

Use this skill whenever external imagery enters the product. License-checked, credited images keep the product shippable and the team out of disputes.

## When to use

- Needing photos, illustrations, or icons.
- Publishing or redistributing a deliverable.
- AI-generated or stock imagery is proposed.

## Procedure

1. Define the need: subject, mood, format, and where it ships.
2. Source from licensed libraries or commissioned work; record license per asset.
3. Verify the license covers the actual use: commercial, redistribution, modification.
4. Attach required credits exactly as the license demands.
5. Prefer generating or commissioning when no license fits.

## Output

Licensed, credited imagery with per-asset license records.

## Pitfalls

- 'Found on the internet' is not a license.
- Credits are legal text, not decoration; place them correctly.
