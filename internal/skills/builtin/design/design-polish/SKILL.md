# Design polish

Use this skill for the final pass that turns 'works' into 'feels right'. Small coordinated adjustments — spacing rhythm, type scale, microcopy, motion — compound into perceived quality.

## When to use

- A design works but feels rough.
- Before a launch, demo, or recording.
- Microcopy confuses or jars.

## Procedure

1. Review spacing rhythm and alignment; fix the inconsistencies, not the system.
2. Tighten the type scale and microcopy: shorter, active, consistent terms.
3. Align colors and elevations with the token system; remove one-off values.
4. Soften or remove motion that distracts; keep what orients.
5. Compare before and after captures side by side before accepting.

## Output

A polished interface with before/after captures and token-conformant tweaks.

## Pitfalls

- Polish never changes behavior or structure; that is redesign.
- One-off values added during polish become tomorrow's debt.
