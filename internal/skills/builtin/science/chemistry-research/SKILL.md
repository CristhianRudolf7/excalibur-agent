# Chemistry research

Use this skill for chemistry done carefully. Correct notation, database grounding, and safety-first interpretation keep computational chemistry useful and lab follow-ups safe.

## When to use

- Chemistry questions need literature or data.
- Planning syntheses or analyses.
- Interpreting computational chemistry.

## Procedure

1. Write structures and reactions in standard notation with conditions.
2. Ground in the canonical databases and check method applicability.
3. Verify safety data for every proposed substance and step.
4. Interpret computations with method limits stated.
5. Record inputs exactly for reproducibility.

## Output

Chemistry results with notation, grounding, and safety notes.

## Pitfalls

- Safety data first; curiosity second.
- Method limits travel with every computed number.
