# Scientific writing

Use this skill for papers, preprints, and talks. Claim-evidence structure, honest figures, and complete methods let reviewers trust and reproduce the work.

## When to use

- Writing or revising a paper or preprint.
- Preparing figures or result slides.
- Responding to reviewers.

## Procedure

1. Structure around claims: each claim followed by its evidence.
2. Prepare figures that stand alone: labeled axes, units, statistics, and sources.
3. Write methods completely enough to reproduce, including negative results.
4. Attribute prior work precisely; distinguish your contribution.
5. Address reviewers point by point with changed text quoted.

## Output

Publication-ready writing with honest figures and full methods.

## Pitfalls

- Figures without statistics mislead; show uncertainty.
- Unattributed prior work is plagiarism; cite generously.
