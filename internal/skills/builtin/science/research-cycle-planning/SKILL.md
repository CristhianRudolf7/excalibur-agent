# Research cycle planning

Use this skill to run research as cycles, not as wandering. Each cycle refines hypotheses through planned experiments with explicit stop rules.

## When to use

- A research program spans multiple experiments.
- Prioritizing what to try next.
- Keeping a team aligned on the research bet.

## Procedure

1. State the current hypotheses and their uncertainties ranked.
2. Plan the cycle's experiments by information value per cost.
3. Define stop rules: what result pivots, persists, or kills each line.
4. Run, consolidate results, and refine hypotheses explicitly.
5. Record the cycle: bets, results, and next hypotheses.

## Output

Planned cycles with bets, stop rules, and recorded outcomes.

## Pitfalls

- Cycles without stop rules drift; define pivots up front.
- Unrecorded results get rerun; log everything.
