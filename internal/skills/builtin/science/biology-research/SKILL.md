# Biology research

Use this skill for biology questions asked properly. Standard nomenclature, appropriate databases, and method awareness keep findings credible and reproducible.

## When to use

- Biology questions need literature or data.
- Working with sequences, structures, or pathways.
- Checking nomenclature or protocols.

## Procedure

1. Use standard nomenclature and identifiers from the start.
2. Query the canonical databases for the data type at hand.
3. Check methods for controls and statistics before trusting a result.
4. Respect biosafety and ethics constraints on any follow-up.
5. Cite accession numbers and versions with every dataset used.

## Output

Biology findings with identifiers, methods checked, and constraints noted.

## Pitfalls

- Names vary; identifiers pin. Use accessions.
- Biosafety and ethics are gates, not footnotes.
