# Video editing

Use this skill for timeline assembly. Ordered cuts, motivated transitions, and corrected color turn footage into a watchable piece.

## When to use

- Assembling footage into a finished video.
- Fixing pacing, order, or color.
- Preparing exports for review or publish.

## Procedure

1. Organize media and assemble the rough cut for story first.
2. Refine cuts to rhythm; transitions motivated, never decorative.
3. Balance layers: main, b-roll, graphics, and captions.
4. Correct color consistently across cameras and scenes.
5. Review the full piece before exporting; fix, don't apologize.

## Output

An assembled, paced, color-consistent edit ready to export.

## Pitfalls

- Effects before story is decoration on confusion.
- Unreviewed exports carry the mistakes to the audience.
