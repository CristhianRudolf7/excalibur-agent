# Simulación química

Use this skill for molecular and reaction questions. Careful method choice and safety-aware interpretation keep simulations useful and grounded.

## When to use

- Reaction outcomes or molecular properties are needed.
- Screening conditions before lab work.
- Interpreting spectra or mechanisms.

## Procedure

1. Define the system, method, and basis appropriate to the question.
2. Respect safety: simulations inform lab work, they do not replace its protocols.
3. Validate against known data points before trusting predictions.
4. Interpret with uncertainty; report method limits plainly.
5. Record inputs so the run reproduces exactly.

## Output

Chemical simulation results with method, validation, and limits.

## Pitfalls

- Method mismatch invalidates results; justify the choice.
- Lab safety protocols always apply to follow-up work.
