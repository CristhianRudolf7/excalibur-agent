# Video audio mixing

Use this skill because audiences forgive shaky video but not bad audio. Synced, leveled, ducked mixes with clean voice keep attention on content.

## When to use

- Mixing dialogue, music, and effects.
- Recording narration for a video.
- Audio quality complaints appear.

## Procedure

1. Sync all audio to picture; fix drift before mixing.
2. Level dialogue first, then duck music and effects under it.
3. Reduce noise and equalize voices for clarity on small speakers.
4. Record narration in a treated space with consistent levels.
5. Check the full mix on headphones and on laptop speakers.

## Output

A clean leveled mix with narration, checked on two outputs.

## Pitfalls

- Music over voice loses viewers; duck ruthlessly.
- Mix on one output only and half the audience suffers.
