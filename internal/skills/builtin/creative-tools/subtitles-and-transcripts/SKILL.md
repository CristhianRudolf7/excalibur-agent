# Subtitles and transcripts

Use this skill to make video accessible and editable. Timestamped transcripts become accurate subtitles, translations, and even an edit decision list.

## When to use

- Subtitling or translating a video.
- Making content accessible or searchable.
- Editing selects from a long recording.

## Procedure

1. Transcribe with timestamps and speaker marks; correct names and terms.
2. Create subtitles respecting reading speed, line length, and sync.
3. Translate with context, not word by word; check timing still fits.
4. Edit the assembly from the transcript: cut by text, verify by picture.
5. Export subtitle files in the required formats alongside the video.

## Output

Accurate subtitles, translations, and transcript-driven edits.

## Pitfalls

- Auto-transcripts err on names; correct them all.
- Overlong lines go unread; respect reading speed.
