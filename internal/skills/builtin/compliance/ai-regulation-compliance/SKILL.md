# AI regulation compliance

Use this skill to map which AI rules apply to the product. Risk class, obligations, and evidence organized early prevent launch-blocking surprises.

## When to use

- Shipping an AI feature in regulated markets.
- Classifying a system under the EU AI Act or similar.
- Planning conformity evidence.

## Procedure

1. Classify the system under the applicable rule's risk tiers.
2. List the obligations for that tier: data, transparency, oversight, logging.
3. Map each obligation to existing or planned evidence.
4. Plan conformity work with owners and deadlines.
5. Track regulatory updates; rules evolve.

## Output

A regulatory map with obligations, evidence, and owners.

## Pitfalls

- Guidance, not counsel; confirm classification with experts.
- Transparency duties apply at deployment; plan them in.
