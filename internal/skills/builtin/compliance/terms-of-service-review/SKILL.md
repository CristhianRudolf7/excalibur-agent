# Terms of service review

Use this skill before agreeing to anything. Plain-language summaries with flagged clauses — data use, lock-in, liability, termination — let the user decide with eyes open.

## When to use

- Adopting a new service or vendor.
- Terms changed under an existing service.
- A contract needs signing.

## Procedure

1. Read the actual terms, not the marketing summary.
2. Summarize in plain language: what you grant, what you owe, what they owe.
3. Flag data use, lock-in, auto-renewal, liability caps, and termination.
4. Compare against alternatives where the stakes justify it.
5. Recommend with the flags; the user decides, counsel advises.

## Output

A plain-language summary with flagged clauses and a recommendation.

## Pitfalls

- Summaries are not counsel; flag when lawyers are needed.
- Auto-renewal and data clauses hide in length; hunt them.
