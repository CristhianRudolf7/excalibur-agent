# Deep research

Use this skill for questions that need real answers. Iterative searching, reading, and contrasting across independent sources produces a cited report with stated limits — not a plausible paragraph.

## When to use

- A decision needs grounded facts.
- Sources disagree or the topic is contested.
- A literature-backed report is the deliverable.

## Procedure

1. Pose the research questions explicitly before searching.
2. Search broadly, then read the best sources fully; record each reading as a source.
3. Contrast claims across independent sources; weight primary evidence over secondary.
4. Deepen where sources conflict or where the decision hinges on a detail.
5. Deliver the report with citations, conclusions, and a limitations section.

## Output

A cited report: findings, conclusions, and honest limitations.

## Pitfalls

- One source is a rumor; corroborate or flag.
- Limits omitted are claims invented; state them.
