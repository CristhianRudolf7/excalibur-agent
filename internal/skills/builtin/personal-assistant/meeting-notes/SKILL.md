# Meeting notes

Use this skill after any recorded meeting. The transcript becomes decisions, owners, and dated tasks — the things people actually need — not a longer document nobody reads.

## When to use

- A meeting transcript or recording exists.
- Someone asks 'what was decided'.
- Follow-ups keep slipping through cracks.

## Procedure

1. Transcribe with speaker marks and timestamps when available.
2. Summarize to decisions first, discussion second, in a few lines.
3. Extract agreements with owners and dates; register tasks and reminders.
4. Flag unresolved items explicitly instead of smoothing them over.
5. Send the notes to attendees and file them where they can be found.

## Output

Notes with summary, decisions, owned tasks, and reminders set.

## Pitfalls

- Decisions without owners do not happen; name one.
- Do not invent agreements the transcript does not support.
