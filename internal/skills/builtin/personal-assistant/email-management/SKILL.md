# Email management

Use this skill for inbox work. Triage and drafts are reversible; sending is not — so everything prepares the send and nothing sends without explicit confirmation.

## When to use

- Triaging or summarizing the inbox.
- Drafting replies for the user.
- Acting on email content (calendar, tasks, files).

## Procedure

1. Classify messages: action needed, waiting on others, FYI, noise.
2. Summarize threads to decisions and open asks, not to message-by-message retellings.
3. Draft replies in the user's voice and length; quote what is being answered.
4. Send only after explicit user confirmation; state recipients and subject when asking.
5. File or archive processed mail so the inbox reflects reality.

## Output

A triaged inbox, summaries, and drafts — sends only with confirmation.

## Pitfalls

- Sending is irreversible; confirm every send explicitly.
- Never invent commitments or dates in a draft.
