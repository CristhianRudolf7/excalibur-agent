# Image editing

Use this skill to fix and compose images precisely. Deliberate edits with before/after comparison keep quality controlled instead of filter-roulette.

## When to use

- Cropping, retouching, or background removal is needed.
- Composing images from parts.
- Preparing images for delivery sizes.

## Procedure

1. Define the target: size, format, and quality bar before editing.
2. Edit non-destructively where the tool allows; keep the original.
3. Check the result at delivery size and on its real background.
4. Compare before and after for unintended changes, especially faces and text.
5. Export with the right format and compression for the use.

## Output

Edited images meeting the target spec with originals kept.

## Pitfalls

- Over-compression destroys delivery; export deliberately.
- Check faces and text closely; artifacts hide there.
