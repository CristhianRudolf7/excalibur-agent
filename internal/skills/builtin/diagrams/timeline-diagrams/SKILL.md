# Timeline diagrams

Use this skill to show time honestly. Dated milestones, durations, and dependencies on one axis turn plans into commitments you can track.

## When to use

- Planning with dates and dependencies.
- Reporting progress against a schedule.
- Coordinating parallel tracks.

## Procedure

1. List milestones with dates and owners, not just phases.
2. Draw durations to scale; dependencies as links, not implications.
3. Mark the critical path and its total float.
4. Show actuals against plan as work proceeds.
5. Flag slips with their downstream impact, not just the delay.

## Output

A scaled timeline with dependencies, owners, and critical path.

## Pitfalls

- Undated phases are wishes; date milestones with owners.
- Hidden dependencies detonate schedules; draw every link.
