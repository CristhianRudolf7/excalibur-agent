# Hierarchy diagrams

Use this skill for tree-shaped information. One root, clear parentage, and balanced depth make hierarchies scannable where prose rambles.

## When to use

- Org charts, taxonomies, or breakdowns are needed.
- Brainstorming needs a mind map.
- Nested prose confuses readers.

## Procedure

1. Fix the single root and the grouping rule for children.
2. Keep depth balanced; promote or merge outlier branches.
3. Label relations where parentage is not obvious.
4. Order siblings by importance or alphabet — deliberately, not randomly.
5. Check the hierarchy against reality with its owner.

## Output

A balanced, labeled hierarchy with a clear grouping rule.

## Pitfalls

- Multiple roots mean multiple diagrams; split them.
- Depth beyond four buries readers; restructure.
