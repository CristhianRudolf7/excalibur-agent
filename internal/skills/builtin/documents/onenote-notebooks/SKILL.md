# OneNote notebooks

Use this skill to make OneNote knowledge findable. Reading section structure and reorganizing around topics beats chronological sprawl.

## When to use

- Finding content across OneNote notebooks.
- Reorganizing notes by topic.
- Migrating notes out of OneNote.

## Procedure

1. Map the notebook: sections, pages, and hierarchy depth.
2. Read target pages fully before reorganizing or quoting.
3. Reorganize by topic with links preserved; keep an index page.
4. Confirm shared-notebook permissions before structural moves.
5. Export with structure intact when migrating.

## Output

Organized notebooks with an index and preserved links.

## Pitfalls

- Structural moves in shared notebooks need confirmation.
- Copy without hierarchy loses the knowledge map.
