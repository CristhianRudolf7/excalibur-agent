# Google Ads

Use this skill for Google Ads work: campaign structure, measurement, and analysis that connects spend to outcomes instead of vanity metrics.

## When to use

- Setting up or auditing ad campaigns.
- Analyzing performance against business goals.
- Controlling spend and attribution.

## Procedure

1. Structure campaigns by intent and audience, with conversion tracking verified first.
2. Set budgets and bids with explicit targets; cap experiments.
3. Analyze by segment: query, device, geography, audience — not by account average.
4. Connect spend to outcomes: revenue or qualified leads, never clicks alone.
5. Document changes and their measured effect; revert losers.

## Output

Campaigns with verified tracking, segmented analysis, and documented changes.

## Pitfalls

- Clicks are not outcomes; optimize for conversions.
- Untracked campaigns spend blindly; verify tracking first.
