# Idea refinement

Use this skill when the user brings a vague idea rather than a task. The goal is a proposal concrete enough to specify: what is in, what is out, what was discarded and why, and the smallest first step that tests the idea.

## When to use

- The user says 'what if we...' or describes a goal without a plan.
- Several directions are possible and the trade-offs are unclear.
- A quick feasibility check is needed before committing work.

## Procedure

1. Restate the idea in one paragraph: problem, beneficiary, and desired outcome.
2. Fix the scope: list what the proposal covers and explicitly what it excludes.
3. Write down 2-3 alternatives considered and why each was discarded or deferred.
4. Name the top risks with a concrete mitigation or test for each.
5. Propose the first step: small, verifiable, and reversible.

## Output

A proposal with scope, alternatives, risks, and a first step; open questions recorded as such, never silently assumed.

## Pitfalls

- Do not jump to implementation; refinement ends with a proposal, not code.
- Do not invent user preferences; mark open choices as open questions.
