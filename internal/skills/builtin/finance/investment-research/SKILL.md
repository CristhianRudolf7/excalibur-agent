# Investment research

Use this skill to research markets and instruments rigorously. Sourced facts with stated limits inform — without crossing into personalized advice, ever.

## When to use

- Researching an asset class, instrument, or market.
- Comparing options with data.
- Understanding risks and mechanics.

## Procedure

1. Define the research question and what would change the view.
2. Gather sourced facts: mechanics, costs, risks, history, with dates.
3. Quantify where possible; state data limits prominently.
4. Compare alternatives on equal terms with the same metrics.
5. Stop at education; never recommend a personal action.

## Output

Sourced research with limits stated and no personal recommendation.

## Pitfalls

- Personalized recommendations are forbidden; educate only.
- Past performance never promises; say what it actually shows.
