# Financial statement analysis

Use this skill to read what statements actually say. Structured analysis with ratios and trends separates healthy from fragile — for learning, not as investment advice.

## When to use

- Understanding a company's financials.
- Comparing statements across periods.
- Learning what ratios reveal.

## Procedure

1. Read all three statements together; they reconcile or something is off.
2. Compute the ratios that matter for the question: liquidity, leverage, profitability.
3. Compare across periods and against peers with context.
4. Flag anomalies: one-offs, restatements, and aggressive policies.
5. Conclude on financial position with the numbers attached.

## Output

A statement analysis with ratios, trends, and flagged anomalies.

## Pitfalls

- Educational analysis, not investment advice; state the boundary.
- One-offs distort; normalize before comparing.
