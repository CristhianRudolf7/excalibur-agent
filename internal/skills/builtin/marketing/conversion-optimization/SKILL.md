# Conversion optimization

Use this skill to turn visitors into users. Friction measured per step, fixed in priority order, and A/B-proven — conversion is a pipeline to instrument, not a page to admire.

## When to use

- Signup or checkout funnels leak.
- Forms get abandoned.
- Landing pages underperform.

## Procedure

1. Instrument the funnel: conversion per step with drop-off counts.
2. Fix the biggest leak first: friction, confusion, or trust.
3. Simplify forms: fewer fields, better defaults, inline validation.
4. A/B test changes that matter; ship winners with significance.
5. Monitor after shipping; regressions hide in redesigns.

## Output

Funnel improvements with measured, significant wins.

## Pitfalls

- Redesigns without funnel data gamble; measure first.
- Insignificant 'wins' are noise; require significance.
