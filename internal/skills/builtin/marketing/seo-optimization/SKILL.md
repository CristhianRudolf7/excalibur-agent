# SEO optimization

Use this skill to earn search traffic honestly. Intent-matched content, technical health, and measured rankings compound — tricks decay.

## When to use

- Pages underperform in search.
- Launching content meant to rank.
- Technical SEO issues are suspected.

## Procedure

1. Match content to the query's intent, not just its keywords.
2. Fix technical basics: speed, structure, metadata, and crawlability.
3. Build depth on topics worth owning; no thin pages.
4. Measure rankings and clicks per page; iterate on losers.
5. Avoid manipulative tactics that risk penalties.

## Output

Optimized pages with intent match and measured movement.

## Pitfalls

- Tricks decay; intent match compounds.
- Measure per page; averages hide the losers.
