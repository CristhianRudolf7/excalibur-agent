# Market research

Use this skill to understand the playing field. Customer evidence plus competitor facts, gathered systematically, ground positioning in reality instead of assumptions.

## When to use

- Entering or repositioning in a market.
- Competitor moves need interpretation.
- Customer understanding is thin.

## Procedure

1. Define the questions the research must answer for a decision.
2. Gather customer evidence: reviews, interviews, usage, complaints.
3. Profile competitors on facts: pricing, features, positioning, momentum.
4. Synthesize into gaps and advantages with the evidence attached.
5. Refresh on schedule; markets move.

## Output

Market findings with customer evidence and competitor facts.

## Pitfalls

- Assumptions disguised as research mislead; label sources.
- Stale research mispositions; refresh it.
