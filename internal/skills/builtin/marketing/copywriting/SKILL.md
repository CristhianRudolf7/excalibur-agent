# Copywriting

Use this skill for words that move people. Audience-first copy with one idea per piece and a clear next step outperforms clever prose that says nothing.

## When to use

- Writing marketing texts, emails, or posts.
- Messaging underperforms.
- Adapting voice to a new channel.

## Procedure

1. Fix the audience, the one idea, and the single action wanted.
2. Draft in the brand voice: short, concrete, active.
3. Cut ruthlessly; every sentence must earn its place.
4. Adapt format to the channel without changing the promise.
5. Test variants where reach justifies it; keep winners with scores.

## Output

Copy with one idea, one action, in brand voice.

## Pitfalls

- Two ideas per piece means zero remembered.
- Channel formats are constraints, not suggestions.
