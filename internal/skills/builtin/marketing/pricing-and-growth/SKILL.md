# Pricing and growth

Use this skill to price and grow deliberately. Value-based packaging, tested offers, and measured loops beat copying competitors and hoping.

## When to use

- Setting or changing prices.
- Designing offers or promotions.
- Growth stalls and tactics scatter.

## Procedure

1. Anchor on customer value and willingness to pay, evidenced not assumed.
2. Package by segments with clear upgrade paths.
3. Test offers with experiments; measure take-up and margin, not just volume.
4. Build one growth loop at a time; measure its cycle before adding more.
5. Review pricing on schedule against costs and competitors.

## Output

Pricing and growth plans with tested offers and measured loops.

## Pitfalls

- Cost-plus pricing ignores value; evidence willingness first.
- Five simultaneous loops measure nothing; sequence them.
