# Ad campaigns

Use this skill to spend on ads deliberately. Structured campaigns with tracked conversions and analyzed segments turn budget into learning, then into returns.

## When to use

- Planning or auditing paid campaigns.
- Spend grows without clear returns.
- Comparing channels or creatives.

## Procedure

1. Define the conversion and its value before spending.
2. Structure by intent with tracked URLs and budgets per segment.
3. Launch small; scale only what converts within target.
4. Analyze by segment weekly; kill losers, feed winners.
5. Report spend versus outcome, never versus clicks.

## Output

Campaigns with tracked conversions and segment analysis.

## Pitfalls

- Untracked spend is donation; track first.
- Scale winners, not averages.
