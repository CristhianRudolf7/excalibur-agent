# Marketing planning

Use this skill to plan marketing as a system. Audience, message, channels, and calendar coordinated — with content to feed them — beats scattered campaigns.

## When to use

- Planning a quarter, launch, or channel mix.
- Content production needs direction.
- Marketing efforts scatter without effect.

## Procedure

1. Fix audience, positioning, and the quarter's one goal.
2. Choose channels by where the audience actually is, with budgets.
3. Build the content calendar feeding each channel deliberately.
4. Produce the visual and video assets the calendar needs.
5. Review metrics per channel; reallocate from losers to winners.

## Output

A coordinated plan with calendar, assets, and review loop.

## Pitfalls

- Everywhere-at-once spreads thin; choose channels.
- Content without distribution is inventory; plan both.
