# Digg search

Use this skill for community-curated interesting stories and discussions. Digg surfaces what engaged readers find worth sharing — a discovery channel, not an authority.

## When to use

- Discovering interesting stories on a topic.
- Reading community reactions and context.
- Finding human-curated links beyond algorithms.

## Procedure

1. Search Digg for the topic and rank by discussion quality.
2. Read comments for context the story omits.
3. Verify factual claims elsewhere before repeating them.
4. Cite the story with its link and date.

## Output

Discovered stories with community context and verification notes.

## Pitfalls

- Curation is taste, not verification; check facts elsewhere.
- Old virals resurface; check dates.
