# Techmeme search

Use this skill for the tech news pulse: what broke, launched, or shifted today, with clustered coverage showing multiple angles at a glance.

## When to use

- Catching up on tech news.
- Checking coverage breadth of one story.
- Finding primary reporting behind a headline.

## Procedure

1. Scan the river and ride pages for the topic.
2. Open the cluster to compare angles across outlets.
3. Prefer primary reporting over aggregation-of-aggregation.
4. Note timestamps; fast-moving stories invalidate older takes.
5. Follow through to source documents when decisions hinge on details.

## Output

News briefs with clustered sources and timestamps.

## Pitfalls

- Headlines compress; read the piece before citing.
- Aggregation is not reporting; find the primary source.
