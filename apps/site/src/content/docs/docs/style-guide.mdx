This page is a visual test fixture, not product documentation. It shows the materials of the manual: a clear reading measure, precise rules, numbered stations, and quiet surfaces. **All commands and records below are sample data.**

## Reading the system

A technical manual should make a complex system easy to inspect. Start with the part you need, follow its connections, and keep the next action in view. The blue rail is a reading aid. It never moves the words you are reading, and the page remains complete when scripts or animations are unavailable.

The normal body face is Inter. Labels, file names and `inline code` use JetBrains Mono. You can **emphasize an instruction**, _name a concept_, or [follow a related page](/docs/) without losing your place. Long identifiers such as `a_very_long_configuration_identifier_with_an_explicit_and_readable_name` stay within the reading column.

## Keep your place

The table of contents follows the current heading. On a phone, use the compact contents menu and the manual index. The reading column keeps the same type, rules and spacing across themes. The line and the station dots are the only strong blue shapes.

The final paragraph makes enough room to check the end of the rail, long-page scrolling, reduced motion and navigation. The footer belongs to Starlight so that previous and next links continue to follow the real documentation order.
